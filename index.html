How this plan works

First every problem with the numbers behind it, then each problem next to its solution, then the phases we will run them in.

What we manage

Island dressing parent B0FT6JXFQ7, US marketplace:

  • 4x4 30ct – hero, B081D9KM88
  • 6x6 30ct – B081DB1T9X
  • 2x2 50ct – B081FJF2LX
  • 4x4 60ct – B0FPPR2SFQ
Baseline we measure against
  • The baseline is the week before we start making changes: 20–26 Oct 2026.
  • Every week is compared with the week before it and with the baseline week.
  • At the end of each phase, the phase is compared with the same number of days just before it started.
  • The 22 Aug – 18 Oct audit window (blended ACOS 43.7%, SP ACOS 49.0%) is the ACOS ceiling we stay under.
Ground rules
  • ACOS stays at or below the audit-window level.
  • Changes are made in phases of fixed length, so a large account is never changed all at once.
  • Nothing is switched off until its replacement has proven itself.

Problem 1

Hero sales are slipping while the account is flat

  • Hero units per day fell from 488 to 418 (−14%) between July and October.
  • Hero sales per day fell from $4,869 to $3,980 (−18%) over the same months.
  • The whole account fell only 3% in that time.
  • Hero conversion (unit session %) stayed at about 60%, so the page still converts – fewer shoppers are reaching it.

Source: Business Reports, Apr – 18 Oct 2026

Solution
  • Problems 2–8 are the causes of this one, so fixing them is how we fix it.
  • We report hero units per day and sales per day every week and at the end of every phase.
Problem 2

The hero lost generic search traffic

  • Hero purchases from generic searches fell from 1,956 to 1,207 a month (−38%) between July and September.
  • "large bandages" fell from 217 to 136 purchases, "bandages" from 167 to 96, "wound care & dressings" from 123 to 48 and "gauze pads" from 83 to 34.
  • Our top-of-search share on "large bandages" fell from about 25% to 7–9%.
  • Search volume on keywords where the hero ranks in the organic top 10 is down 63% since June.

Source: SQP ASIN view; SP Targeting report; Helium 10 Cerebro

Solution
  • We give the generic searches their own hero campaigns, with their own budget and a 50% ACOS ceiling.
  • We bid these to the top of search, where they convert best (47% conversion on top of search vs 26% on product pages).
  • We also run the 60ct on generic terms where it brings in more sales per click than the hero.

Problem 3

Source: Helium 10; SP Search Term, Targeting and Impression Share reports; SQP brand view

Solution

  • The 6x6 and 60ct also bid on "island dressing" – they bring in $8.65 and $10.27 per click, so they can pay more and stay inside target.
  • The 60ct goes on YHOVID’s own product pages as the value option.

  • A clean discovery campaign keeps finding new search terms.

Problem 7

Bids and placements are not tied to results

  • Every campaign uses "Dynamic bids – down only", so Amazon lowers our bids when top of search gets expensive.
  • The top-of-search modifier is a flat +30–50% on most campaigns.
  • Product pages take 13% of spend at 64% ACOS, while top of search runs at 46% ACOS with 47% conversion.
  • An Amazon Business modifier of +60–100% has never been measured.
  • The hero pays $4.25 per click on "island dressing", where a click brings in $5.83 of sales – 73% ACOS.

Source: SP Placement report; bulk file; SP Search Term report

Solution
  • Key campaigns move to fixed bids.
  • Every base bid = ACOS target × the keyword’s sales per click ÷ (1 + placement modifier), so even a top-of-search click stays within target.
  • Keyword campaigns get +30–50% on top of search, 0% on product pages, and 0% Amazon Business until we can measure it.
  • Modifiers are reviewed every two weeks and moved by at most 10 points at a time.

  • The 60ct brings in $10.27 per click on "island dressing" but has no role on the head term or as a value pack.

Source: SP Advertised Product and Search Term reports

Solution
  • The 6x6 and 2x2 get their own size-term campaigns.
  • The existing 6x6 auto campaign (31% ACOS) stays.
  • The 60ct becomes the value pack on head terms, generic terms and competitor pages.

Phase 027 Oct – 2 Nov · 1 week

Set-up and quick wins

  • Get Advertising console access and connect Scale Insights, so hourly data starts collecting.
  • Send the baseline report (20–26 Oct) so every later week has a clear starting point.
  • Add the 34 negative keywords and negate our own woven gauze in the island-dressing campaigns.
  • Raise the Sponsored Brands bids on the head terms by one step (+15–20%).
  • Agree the promotion decisions (tailored promotion test, Subscribe & Save coupon).
  • No new keyword campaigns are launched in this phase.

To move on: Tracking is live and the baseline report has been sent.

Phase 13 – 23 Nov · 3 weeks

Head term and hero foundation

  • Launch the head-term and niche campaigns for the hero (H1, H2, H3) and the branded campaign (H6).
  • Launch the 6x6 size-term and head-term campaigns (S1, S2) and the 60ct value head-term campaign (V1).
  • Launch own-page defense (F1) and the dedicated Sponsored Brands head-term campaign (B1).
  • Add the new exact keywords as negatives in the 6x6 auto and the legacy auto campaigns.
  • Trim the legacy campaigns’ budgets by the same amount the new campaigns spend, so total spend stays flat; their keywords are not touched yet.
  • Start the tailored promotion test and the Subscribe & Save coupon (if approved), and submit Black Friday / Cyber Monday deals by 13 Nov.

To move on: Every Phase 1 campaign has at least 14 days of data.

Phase BF24 Nov – 1 Dec · 8 days

Black Friday / Cyber Monday

  • No new campaigns and no structural changes during the event.
  • Budgets are topped up only on campaigns running at or under their ACOS target – first on the sizes that are on deal.
  • The event is reported separately, so it does not distort the phase-over-phase comparison.

To move on: The event is over.

Phase 22 – 22 Dec · 3 weeks

Generic recovery and competitor pages

  • Scale the Phase 1 winners: budget +20% a week for every campaign at or under target with 10+ orders.
  • Launch the hero generic campaigns (H4, H5) and the 60ct generic campaign (V2).
  • Launch competitor-page targeting by size (H7, H8, S3, T2, V3) and the 2x2 size-term campaign (T1).
  • Launch Sponsored Display on competitor pages and to recent viewers (D1, D2), and Sponsored Brands on generic terms (B3).
  • In the legacy campaigns: negate the keywords whose new owner has 14 days at target, cut budgets 20% a week, archive the dead keywords, and step the Amazon Business modifier down to +30%.
  • Run the first harvest: move proven search terms to exact.

To move on: Every Phase 2 campaign has at least 14 days of data.

Phase 323 Dec – 12 Jan · 3 weeks

Consolidate and optimise

  • Scale the Phase 2 winners the same way.
  • Launch the hero discovery campaign (H9), the 2x2 auto (T3) and Sponsored Brands video (B2) if the video is ready.
  • Bring the legacy campaigns down to the low-bid catch-all (~$115 a day) and pause the campaigns that have been replaced.
  • Start dayparting, using 7+ weeks of hourly data.
  • Review all placement modifiers.
  • Start the retention promotion and the Creator Connections test.

To move on: About 90% of spend runs in the new structure.

Phase 4From 13 Jan · Ongoing, reviewed every 3 weeks

Scale

  • Every three weeks, move budget to the campaigns that performed best in the last period.
  • Spend only goes above today’s level if ACOS stays at or below the ceiling and the brand approves.

To move on: Steady state.

Why three weeks: two weeks give each new campaign enough clicks to judge it fairly, and the third week is used to act on what we see. Spend stays at today’s daily level in every phase. Within a phase, money only moves from the legacy campaigns to the new ones as fast as the new ones hit their ACOS targets.

How "before" and "after" are defined
  • Baseline: the week before we start making changes, 20–26 Oct 2026.
  • Every week: this week vs last week, and vs the baseline week.
  • End of each phase: the whole phase vs the same number of days just before it started (e.g. Phase 1’s 21 days vs the 21 days before 3 Nov). Read 3 days after the phase ends, once ads attribution has settled.
  • New campaigns are also compared with the legacy campaigns running alongside them, to separate our effect from seasonality.
  • Promotion weeks (the hero coupon runs 22 Oct – 5 Nov), creative test windows and stock issues are flagged so they are not counted as PPC effects. For Phases 2–3 we also compare with Nov–Dec 2025 Business Reports.
Winners, losers, and what happens next
  • Winner: at or under its ACOS target with 10+ orders in the phase → budget +20% a week through the next phase; bids recalculated upward if ACOS is 10+ points under target.
  • Needs work: over target by up to 10 points → bids recalculated, kept in the next phase.
  • Loser: over target by 10+ points after 14 days → bids cut; if still over at the end of the next phase, merged into the catch-all.
  • Every phase ends with a phase report: before vs after, winners scaled, losers fixed, and what launches next.
